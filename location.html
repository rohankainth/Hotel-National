Getting Here

From New Delhi Railway Station to Hotel National

Arriving by train? Here's the easiest way to reach us.

On Foot

Exit towards the Paharganj / Ajmeri Gate side of NDLS — Hotel National is an easy walk of just a few minutes along Arakashan Road.

By Auto-Rickshaw / Cab

A short, inexpensive ride from the station forecourt — just show the driver our address or let us share live location on WhatsApp.

By Metro

New Delhi Metro Station (Yellow & Airport Express lines) is close by, with the hotel a short walk from the Paharganj-side exit.

First time in Paharganj? Message us on WhatsApp when you arrive at the station and we'll guide you to the hotel, step by step.
